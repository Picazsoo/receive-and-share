# Receive & Share for Kobo Libra 2, version 0.1

First release (prerelease).

I made this so my wife could put books on her Kobo Libra 2 without a USB cable from either a phone or a computer.

Send EPUB and PDF files from a phone or computer over local Wi-Fi, using a browser.
You can also browse book folders and download individual books or a ZIP. A basic
browser view is provided for Kobo-to-Kobo sharing.

[Screenshots](https://github.com/Picazsoo/receive-and-share/blob/main/docs/screenshots/README.md)

- Upload EPUB and PDF files from a browser, with a queue and progress per file.
- Browse book folders and download individual books or a ZIP.
- Choose specific books to share, or share all. Nothing is shared by default.
- Connect using a QR code, a short pairing code, or a configurable `.local` name.
- Basic browser view for Kobo-to-Kobo sharing.

## Install

First [exclude application folders from My Books](https://github.com/Picazsoo/receive-and-share/blob/main/docs/USAGE.md#keep-application-files-out-of-my-books).
Kobo can otherwise index support files inside `.adds` as books.

Download and extract `Receive-Share-v0.1.zip`. Choose one folder:

| Already installed | Folder |
| --- | --- |
| NickelMenu and NickelDBus | `app-only` |
| NickelMenu only | `with-nickeldbus` |
| Neither | `first-install` |

Copy that folder's `KoboRoot.tgz` into the Kobo's `.kobo` folder, without extracting
or renaming it. Safely eject, unplug, and wait for installation and restart.
Open **NickelMenu → Receive & Share**.

Settings, received books and other menu entries are preserved. Dependency bundles
replace the included dependency binaries; choose them only for missing dependencies.

## Testing and limits

Tested on Kobo Libra 2 with firmware 4.38.23697. Other devices, iOS, and download/import
on a second Kobo still need testing. NickelMenu currently does not support firmware 5.x.
The app was tested through SSH deployment; installation through the Kobo updater is
not yet device-tested.

Keep the Kobo awake and both devices on the same Wi-Fi. Transfers use unencrypted
local HTTP. Uploads are limited to 100 MiB each; sessions last 20 minutes.

Forks, PRs and testing on other Kobo readers are welcome. Please include your model
and firmware version when reporting results.
